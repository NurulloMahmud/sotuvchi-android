**Full description** (1621/4000)

```
Sotuvchi is the owner's app for shops that sell through Instagram.

Customers write to your Instagram. The AI seller answers them, negotiates the price within the limits you set, takes the order and asks for a payment receipt. You check the receipt, approve the sale and keep selling, from your phone.

ORDERS
- See orders that need you first: receipts to check, open orders, approved and rejected sales.
- Open the payment receipt, zoom in, and compare the amount the AI read from it with the order total.
- Approve, reject with a reason, or cancel an order.

CHATS
- Read every conversation the AI handles, and see which ones need you.
- Pause the AI and answer by hand, then hand the chat back.
- Reply within Instagram's 24-hour window, with a clear countdown.

PRODUCTS AND STORES
- Manage your catalogue: photos, price, the lowest price the AI may accept, stock, categories and variants such as size and color.
- Switch the AI on or off for a store, edit its instructions, set the payment card, stock hold time and pickup address.
- Get order alerts on Telegram.

HOME
- Approved sales for today, 7 days, this month or all time, with the change against the previous period.
- A daily revenue chart, top products and comment-campaign results.

TEAM
- Invite admins and members. Members see everything but cannot change anything.

Uzbek, Russian and English. Sign in with a code sent to your email; the same code creates your account if you are new.

Sotuvchi needs a Sotuvchi workspace with a connected Instagram Business or Creator account. Set that up once at sotuvchi.ai. Subscriptions are managed on the website.
```
